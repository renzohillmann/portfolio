## Projects

### Climate Change in Colombia: A Case Study of Temperature Trends in Four Distinct Locations (Data Science in Python)

[Publication](https://renzohillmann.github.io/portfolio/)

![Results](/assets/proyect2.PNG)

The temperature increase was identified at four distinct locations in Colombia using **Python**, each exhibiting unique climatic characteristics. The findings suggest that temperatures may be increasing at a faster rate in regions with colder and warmer climates compared to those with moderate climates.

In regions with lower temperatures, such as high mountains (*Nevados*, páramos, and glaciers, including those observed in La Sierra Nevada del Cocuy), temperature changes appear more pronounced than in other locations. In El Cocuy, for instance, an increase of 6°C in the daily maximum temperature was recorded.

### Site Percolation and Threshold Analysis Using Union-Find Algorithms in C++

![Results](/assets/Percolation.JPG)

We investigated the probability that a percolating cluster exists in a randomly filled square lattice of size L. The Hoshen–Kopelman algorithm was adapted to identify and label connected clusters in the lattice, distinguish them from one another, and determine whether a percolating cluster exists.
